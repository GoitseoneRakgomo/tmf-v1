Privacy Policy & POPIA Compliance

Commitment to Data Protection

The Thuli Madonsela Foundation Trust (Registration IT000570/2017(G)) is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal information is processed safely and legally in compliance with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) of South Africa.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us, including when you:

  • Register for events, summits, or leadership courses.
  • Make a donation or request a Section 18A tax certificate.
  • Subscribe to research publications, policy briefs, or newsletters.
  • Submit general or media inquiries through our contact channels.

2. How We Use Your Personal Information

Your personal data is strictly processed for the intended institutional purposes, such as issuing Section 18A certificates, confirming event registrations, providing research updates, and maintaining legal compliance.

3. Section 18A & Financial Security

Sensitive details requested for tax certificates (ID/Passport numbers, Tax Reference numbers) are handled with elevated privacy protections. We do not retain unneeded financial records or share donor credentials with external commercial third parties.

4. Your POPIA Rights

Under POPIA, you have the right to request access to your personal data held by the Foundation, request corrections or updates, or object to/opt out of direct communications at any time.
