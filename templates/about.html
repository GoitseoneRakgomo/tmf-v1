Context & Mandate

Why We Exist

The Foundation responds to structural inequality, exclusion from democracy's benefits, and a growing public trust deficit. We address societal polarisation, governance failure, and civic apathy among young people by providing forward-looking thinking about democracy for 21st-century challenges.

Vision

An impactful think-and-action tank for sustainable democracy, the rule of law and peace.

Mission

A one-stop civic empowerment think-and-action tank providing leadership solutions for sustainable democracy, development and peace.

Purpose

We empower through democracy leadership solutions that deepen, defend and make democracy work for all.

The Founder

Prof. Thuli Madonsela

The Foundation's work is rooted in the biography and public-service record of Prof. Thuli Madonsela. As a private, non-partisan, and operational institution, our framework relies on her commitment to ethical, purposeful, impactful, and service-committed leadership.

Governance & Leadership

Our Team & Board of Trustees

The Foundation is governed by a dedicated Board of Trustees and managed by an executive team focused on operational excellence. Please note the team structure reflects only current staff, as past members Catherine Peter and Belinda Moses are no longer with the Foundation.

Legacy & Transparency

Archive & Annual Reports

We are currently building a permanent, searchable public record of Prof. Madonsela's public-service work and the Foundation's institutional output. If you hold relevant materials, we invite you to contact us.

To uphold donor credibility, we also publish our Annual Reports and Financials, ensuring full institutional transparency.
