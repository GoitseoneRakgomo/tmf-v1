PAIA Manual & Information Access

Promotion of Access to Information Act (PAIA)

In accordance with Section 51 of the Promotion of Access to Information Act 2 of 2000 (PAIA), this manual details the records held by the Thuli Madonsela Foundation Trust and the procedures for requesting access.

1. Foundation Details

  • Entity Name: Thuli Madonsela Foundation Trust
  • Trust Registration: IT000570/2017(G)
  • Physical Address: 430 Lea Street, Waterkloof Glen, Pretoria, South Africa

2. Categories of Records Held

Subject to the provisions of PAIA and POPIA, the Foundation maintains records in the following categories:

  • Institutional Records: Trust deed, governance policies, and board meeting minutes.
  • Financial Records: Audited annual financial statements, tax records, and Section 18A receipts.
  • Research & Operational Records: Publications, policy submissions, and event records.

3. Requesting Access

To request access to a record, a requester must complete the prescribed Form 2 under PAIA regulations and submit it to our Information Officer via email, accompanied by proof of payment for any applicable request fees.
